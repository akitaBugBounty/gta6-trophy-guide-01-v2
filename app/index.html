    Roadmap to the Platinum

    One playthrough, zero online trophies, about 200 hours. Work the stages in order: story first (with the one missable done in Chapter 1), partners and side stories next, then robberies, then the long grind, then money and clean-up, and finally the 100% checklist that unlocks the platinum.

    Save rules for the whole run: make a manual save at the start of every chapter (15 slots); never use cheats on your trophy save — cheats disable trophies and saving on that save; keep a full save from before the final mission, because there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus.

      Missables

      Only one trophy is confirmed missable, and one strand is time-sensitive. Everything else stays available after the credits.

      After the credits

      Every side-story finale, robbery, collectible and activity can still be finished once the story is over. The 100% checklist only appears in Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion after the story ends, but progress made earlier still counts toward it.
